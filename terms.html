About FindSpot

FindSpot is a free fieldwork and recording platform for UK metal detectorists. It includes FieldGuide™, a proprietary landscape intelligence engine, permission management, find recording, and club and rally tools.

FindSpot is provided free of charge. No account is required. Your finds, permissions and landowner details are stored locally on your own device and never transmitted to our servers.

FieldGuide

FieldGuide™ uses proprietary methods to support landscape interpretation. It combines terrain, LiDAR, satellite imagery, historic mapping, environmental data, hydrology and archaeological context to help identify areas of likely past activity.

FindSpot does not claim ownership of any third-party source datasets. The selection, combination, sequencing, transformation, weighting, scoring, classification and generated interpretation of those datasets within FieldGuide remain the intellectual property of FindSpot.

FieldGuide outputs are provided for field research support only. They highlight areas of potential, not guaranteed finds.

Report Use

Generated FindSpot reports — including field visit reports, find cards and permission agreements — may be shared freely for personal detecting records, landowner communications, club reporting and heritage recording purposes.

Commercial reproduction of report layouts or generated interpretation without written permission is not permitted.

Data and Privacy

FindSpot is local-first. Your find spots, permissions and landowner details are stored on your device only and are never uploaded to our servers.

Club and rally dig data is only transmitted when you choose to send it, using the in-app export feature. See our Privacy page for full details.

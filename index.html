FAQ

Common questions

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. FindSpot is a Progressive Web App, so it works in a modern phone browser. On iPhone, open it in Safari and use Add to Home Screen. On Android, your browser will usually offer to install it.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no account, login or email required. You can start using FindSpot immediately.

Does it use mobile data in the field?

Find recording, permission management and trail recording work offline. FieldGuide needs a connection to fetch landscape data for a new area, but loaded results and map tiles can be cached.

What if I lose or replace my phone?

FindSpot supports explicit, user-controlled backups and reminds you when changing records need protection. Save the backup somewhere safe, then use FindSpot's validated restore tools on your replacement device. Recovery is not automatic cloud sync.

Is it really free?

Yes. FindSpot is free to use in full, with no adverts, subscription, locked tier or account requirement.

What is FieldGuide?

FieldGuide analyses terrain, water features, historic routes and recorded archaeology — including Portable Antiquities Scheme data and your own locally recorded finds — and shows the evidence behind its interpretations. It helps focus a search, but it does not guarantee finds.

What if I find something important?

FindSpot includes guided workflows for Stop & Secure, Map Scatter and Notable Find records. They help preserve context and support responsible recording, but they do not replace professional or legal advice.

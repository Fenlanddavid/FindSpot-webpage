Built to reject weak signals

Most apps show everything — inflating confidence and overwhelming users with noise. FieldGuide is designed differently.

The aim is not to highlight everything.
The aim is to help you focus on the signals that matter.

This is one of FindSpot's core strengths: it is selective by design.

FieldGuide suppresses:

  • Modern disturbance Recent cultivation, drainage works and ground disturbance downweighted before results are surfaced.
  • Drainage patterns Modern field drainage can look like historic hydrology. FieldGuide helps separate the two.
  • Weak single-source anomalies A single signal is not enough. Isolated anomalies are filtered unless corroborated by independent evidence.
  • Misleading terrain noise Not every terrain feature is archaeologically meaningful. FieldGuide is designed to reduce false confidence.

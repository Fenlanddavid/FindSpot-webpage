FieldGuide

Choose where to detect before you arrive.

FieldGuide reads terrain, water, routes, historic mapping and nearby context to highlight areas where past activity is more plausible.

  • Reads terrain morphologySurface shape and micro-topography are analysed for clues that are easy to miss from ground level.
  • Reveals lost boundaries and routesHistoric Ordnance Survey mapping helps you spot vanished tracks, old field edges and settlement clues.
  • Highlights convergence zonesWater, routes and landscape edges are stronger when they point to the same area.
  • Includes protected landscape awarenessScheduled monument boundaries are visible before you are standing on them.

FieldGuide highlights potential and helps focus your search. It does not guarantee finds.

FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before you install.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. FindSpot is a Progressive Web App, so it works in a modern phone browser. On iPhone, open it in Safari and use Add to Home Screen. On Android, your browser will usually offer to install it.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no account, login or email required. You can start using FindSpot immediately.

Does it use mobile data in the field?

Find recording, permission management and coverage tracking work offline. FieldGuide needs a connection to fetch landscape data for a new area, but loaded results and map tiles can be cached.

Is it really free?

Yes. FindSpot is free to use in full, with no subscription, locked tier or account requirement.

What is FieldGuide?

FieldGuide is FindSpot's landscape intelligence engine. It analyses terrain, water features, historic movement corridors and archaeological context to suggest stronger starting points. It helps focus a search, but it does not guarantee finds.

What if I find something important?

FindSpot includes guided workflows for Stop & Secure, Map Scatter and Notable Find records. They help preserve context and support responsible recording, but they do not replace professional or legal advice.
